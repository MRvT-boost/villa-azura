Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2025

Villa Azura is a private holiday rental owned by Mirjam & Meinoud Rost van Tonningen, based in the Netherlands. We take your privacy seriously and are committed to protecting your personal data in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. Who We Are

This website is operated by Mirjam & Meinoud Rost van Tonningen, owners of Villa Azura, Carrer la Morena 11, 03720 Benissa, Alicante, Spain. You can contact us at azurabenissacosta@gmail.com.

2. What Data We Collect

We only collect personal data that you provide voluntarily through our contact form. This includes:

  • Your first and last name
  • Your email address
  • Your intended arrival and departure dates
  • Number of guests
  • Any message you choose to include

We do not collect any data automatically, we do not use analytics tools, and we do not track your behaviour on this website.

3. How We Use Your Data

The data you submit via the contact form is used solely to respond to your enquiry about renting Villa Azura. We will never use your data for marketing purposes, sell it to third parties, or share it with anyone other than as described below.

4. Third-Party Services

This website uses the following third-party services which may set cookies or process data:

  • Formspree — processes contact form submissions and forwards them to our email. See Formspree's privacy policy.
  • Google Calendar — used to display our availability calendar. Google may set cookies when the calendar is loaded. This is only activated after you accept cookies. See Google's privacy policy.
  • Google Fonts — used to load typography for this website. Your IP address may be transmitted to Google servers. See Google's privacy policy.

5. Cookies

This website uses cookies only when you explicitly consent to them. The cookies we use are:

  • cookieConsent — a cookie stored in your browser to remember your cookie preference. This cookie does not track you and contains no personal data.
  • Google Calendar cookies — set by Google when the availability calendar is displayed. Only active after you accept cookies.

You can withdraw your cookie consent at any time by clearing your browser's cookies and local storage.

6. Data Retention

Enquiry data submitted via the contact form is retained in our email inbox for as long as necessary to handle your enquiry. We do not store your data in any database or CRM system.

7. Your Rights

Under GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Object to processing of your data
  • Lodge a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens) at autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at azurabenissacosta@gmail.com.

8. Security

This website is served over HTTPS, ensuring that all data transmitted between your browser and our website is encrypted. Contact form submissions are processed by Formspree over a secure connection.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date at the top.
